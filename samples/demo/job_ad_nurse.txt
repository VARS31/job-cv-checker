Registered Nurse - Surgical Ward (m/f/d) - St. Elmar Clinic, Leipzig
(Fictional demo job ad. The clinic does not exist.)

Our 32-bed surgical ward cares for patients before and after planned and emergency operations.

Your responsibilities
- Plan and deliver nursing care for surgical patients.
- Monitor vital signs, give medication and care for wounds.
- Document care in our electronic patient record.
- Work closely with doctors, physiotherapists and the social service.

Your profile
- Completed nursing training with state exam, or an equivalent foreign qualification recognised in Germany.
- German at level B2 or higher.
- Valid Basic Life Support (BLS) certificate.
- Willingness to work in three shifts, including weekends and holidays.
- Ideally 2 years of experience on a surgical ward.
- Experience in intensive care is a plus.

We offer a permanent contract and paid further training.
